About Cal Golden Bars

Cal Golden Bars helps Cal fans find each other on game day.

Find Cal Bars — locations with persistent Cal communities — along with fan-added locations where you may find a fellow Bear watching the game.

Planning to watch at your local bar? Mark “I’ll be here” so other Bears know where to find you.

Organizing a gathering for a specific game? Add a Watch Party so fans nearby can join you.

Own or manage a bar hosting a Cal event? Contact Cal Golden Bars.
