From screen to somewhere

Connected online.
Not together enough.

We spend more time connected through screens than ever, but that does not always translate into spending more time together.

Group chats, feeds, event pages, and online communities can tell us who shares our interests. They are often much worse at answering a simpler question: where can I actually go to be around these people?

CrowdMapped uses the screen as a starting point rather than the destination.

The model

Three things make a community visible.

CrowdMapped separates persistent places, specific activity, and participation signals so the map can become more useful as a community uses it.

01 / Place

Places

Persistent real-world locations where members of a community gather.

02 / Activity

Activity

Specific gatherings connected to a game, date, event, or occasion.

03 / People

People

Participation signals that show where a community is actually coming together.

The first CrowdMapped community

Cal Golden Bars

Cal Golden Bars is the first implementation of CrowdMapped. It helps Cal fans find where other Bears are gathering for a particular game, discover organized watch parties, find recurring Cal bars, add new locations, and tell other fans where they plan to watch.

Cal Golden Bars is built around college football. CrowdMapped is built around the broader problem of helping people find one another and gather in person.
